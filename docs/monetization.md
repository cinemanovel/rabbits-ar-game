# Monetization

The app is freemium, but monetization must not damage trust or mystery fairness.

## Monetization Principles

- Free players should understand and enjoy the core mystery.
- Paid content may deepen immersion, expand story, or improve convenience.
- Do not sell access to safety-critical information.
- Do not create pay-to-win community status.
- Keep prices, renewal terms, and restore behavior clear.

## RevenueCat Later

RevenueCat is planned later. Do not install or integrate it until the monetization phase is approved.

When approved, use RevenueCat for:

- Subscription or entitlement state.
- Purchase restoration.
- Cross-platform purchase handling.
- Server-side entitlement validation where needed.

## Possible Premium Value

Potential paid value should stay aligned with the fiction:

- Bonus case files.
- Expanded archive access.
- Cinematic audio or visual layers.
- Optional deeper analysis tools.
- Cosmetic interface themes that remain restrained.
- Early access to side stories, not unfair core solutions.

## Avoid

- Loot boxes.
- Paid random rewards.
- Aggressive countdown pressure.
- Selling exact puzzle answers.
- Paywalled safety fallbacks.
- Dark patterns around cancellation or restore purchases.

## Implementation Rules

Before adding purchases:

1. Define free vs paid value in plain language.
2. Define entitlement names.
3. Define restore purchases UX.
4. Define refund/support path.
5. Define server-side validation needs.
6. Verify App Store and Google Play compliance.
