# Safety and Moderation

Safety is a product requirement, not an afterthought. This is especially important because the app is location-aware, mystery-driven, and community-oriented.

## Safety Principles

- Never encourage trespassing, stalking, dangerous travel, or unsafe meetups.
- Avoid gameplay that pressures players to visit isolated places or act at unsafe times.
- Provide alternatives for players who cannot use camera, location, sound, or motion.
- Keep permission explanations honest and non-lore-obscured.
- Treat player reports and moderation records as sensitive.

## Location Safety

Location-aware features must:

- Prefer public, safe, accessible places.
- Avoid private property and restricted areas.
- Avoid requiring nighttime visits.
- Use approximate geofences unless precise location is truly necessary.
- Include a remote or alternate path for important progression.

## Community Safety

Before user-generated content ships, require:

- Report content or user.
- Moderator review process.
- Content removal process.
- Abuse escalation path.
- Audit log.
- Player-facing rules.

## Content Safety

Mystery content may be tense, strange, and cinematic, but should avoid:

- Real-world threats that could be mistaken for emergencies.
- Instructions for illegal activity.
- Content that impersonates authorities.
- Targeting real private individuals.
- Graphic gore or exploitative shock.

## Incident Response

For future production operations, define:

1. Who reviews urgent reports.
2. How content can be disabled quickly.
3. How unsafe locations are removed.
4. How players are notified if needed.
5. How moderation actions are audited.

## Developer Rule

If a feature touches location, community, identity, purchases, or user-generated content, pause and check this document before implementation.
