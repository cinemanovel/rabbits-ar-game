# Community Rules

The community is part of the game, but it must be designed safely. Do not add broad social features without moderation support.

## Community Principles

- Community features should increase mystery, cooperation, and interpretation.
- The app should not reward harassment, doxxing, trespassing, or unsafe meetups.
- Public content should be limited until reporting, review, blocking, and moderation flows exist.
- Younger or vulnerable players must not be pushed toward private contact or risky travel.

## Allowed Early Community Patterns

These are safer early patterns:

- Read-only official broadcasts.
- Curated community theories.
- Limited emoji-style reactions.
- Anonymous aggregate progress.
- Event participation counts.
- Staff-selected puzzle hints.

## Avoid Until Reviewed

Do not add these without a moderation plan:

- Open text feeds.
- Public profiles with detailed personal data.
- Private messaging.
- Group creation.
- Photo or video uploads.
- User-created location markers.
- Real-time chat.
- Reputation systems.

## Player Conduct Baseline

Future community surfaces should enforce:

- No harassment or hate.
- No threats or encouragement of self-harm.
- No doxxing or sharing private personal data.
- No trespassing instructions.
- No impersonation of staff, characters, or emergency services.
- No sexual content or exploitation.
- No spam, scams, or off-platform solicitation.

## Moderation Requirements

Before user-generated content ships, the app needs:

1. Report flow.
2. Block or mute flow where social contact exists.
3. Moderator review queue.
4. Content removal path.
5. Audit trail for moderation actions.
6. Clear player-facing rules.
